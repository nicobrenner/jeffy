Turn 3: does best-of-4 hold up, or does it get canned?

Twelve 3-turn conversations on your pick (ct2). Turns 1 and 2 are shared history (turn 2 came from best-of-4, so you can see what it chose); the two replies are turn 3 from the SAME model: one is a single sample, the other is the best of four picked by the judge retrained on your 80 scores. Score each 0–5 and say in the note whether the conversation feels canned by now (that is the thing this page measures). Which is which is hidden.

0 = broken/wrong/offensive · 1 = flat or off · 2 = passable · 3 = fine · 4 = good, in character · 5 = exactly what you'd want. Notes are optional and worth more than the number. Progress is saved in this browser; export at the bottom.
